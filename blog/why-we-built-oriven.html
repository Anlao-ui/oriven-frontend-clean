Why we built OrivenAI

By the OrivenAI teamPublished 13 July 2026Updated 14 October 20265 min read

On this page

  1. The problems we kept seeing
  2. The principles behind OrivenAI
  3. Who it’s for
  4. Where we are now

Updated in October 2026.

Running ads as a small team means doing a specialist’s job in between everything else. You research the market in one place, write copy in another, design creative in a third, set up campaigns in each ad platform’s own manager, and check results in yet another set of dashboards. Every switch costs time, and every tool knows only a slice of your business.

We built OrivenAI because we kept seeing the same problems in that setup, and believed they could be solved in one workspace.

The problems we kept seeing

Every tool starts from zero

Each tool asks what you sell and who you sell to, and none of them remembers. Ads generated without that context come out generic, and editing them back into your voice takes as long as writing them.

Creative and campaigns live apart

A good ad is only useful once it’s in a correctly configured campaign. Getting from “we have creative” to “it’s live with the right objective, audience and budget” is where many small advertisers lose days, or launch half-configured campaigns.

Every platform works differently

Google, Meta, TikTok and Pinterest each have their own structure, formats and rules. Doing the same work separately for each platform multiplies the effort; our guide on managing multiple ad platforms describes how much discipline that takes.

Automation without limits is scary

Automated changes can save time, but many people don’t trust them, for good reason: an automated rule that changes budgets or pauses campaigns at the wrong moment costs real money.

The principles behind OrivenAI

  • One business profile, used everywhere. You describe your business once in Control Center, and Research, Create and every other step build on it.
  • Respect each platform’s structure. Create builds campaigns the way each ad platform expects, instead of forcing one format on all of them.
  • You stay in control of what goes live. Launch shows whether a draft is ready, and campaigns are created paused in your own ad account so you review them before they run.
  • Automation within limits you set. Autopilot only applies rules you define, on Google Ads and Meta Ads.
  • No invented numbers. Performance comes from the ad platforms themselves, predictions are labeled as predictions, and Research shows its sources.

Who it’s for

OrivenAI is for small businesses, founders and marketers who run their own ads and want one workspace for the whole process: research, creative, launch, results and routine decisions. It isn’t trying to replace your analytics tool or every specialist tool an agency might use; it’s trying to make the core loop of advertising faster and calmer for small teams.

Where we are now

OrivenAI supports Google Ads, Meta Ads, TikTok Ads and Pinterest Ads today. You can read what each part does in What you can do with OrivenAI, or learn more about the category in What is an AI advertising platform? If you have feedback, we’d like to hear it at contact@orivenai.com.
