PPC automation

PPC automation software that keeps you in control

OrivenAI Automations schedule your campaigns and watch their performance, then act on the rules you write: pause what’s wasting budget, resume what recovers, adjust budget within limits, or simply tell you and wait for your approval.

Why teams automate PPC, and where it goes wrong

Most paid-media work is repetitive: checking whether a campaign’s cost per acquisition drifted overnight, pausing an ad set that stopped converting, nudging budget toward the campaign that is working. Doing that by hand across platforms means either spending hours in dashboards or reacting a day late.

Automation fixes the speed problem but creates a control problem. Opaque “AI optimizers” can change budgets you never meant to touch, and stacking several automations on one account makes it hard to know why something changed. OrivenAI takes the opposite approach: every automated action comes from a rule you can read, with limits you set, and an activity log of what happened.

What OrivenAI automates

Conditions

Rules on real campaign metrics

Build conditions on spend, impressions, clicks, CTR, CPC, conversions, CPA, ROAS or campaign status, for example “CPA above €40 over the last 7 days”. Missing data is never treated as zero, and rules wait for enough data before acting.

Actions

Pause, resume and adjust budgets

When a rule matches, an automation can pause or resume a campaign, or increase or decrease its daily budget. Schedules switch campaigns on, pause, resume or stop them at the times you choose.

Approval

Notify or ask first

Prefer to decide yourself? A rule can notify you or request approval instead of acting, so automation never outruns your judgment.

Limits

Built-in guardrails

Budget rules need a maximum budget and are limited per change, per day and over several days. Creating an automation costs 20 credits once; its checks and changes don’t use credits.

Visibility

An activity log

Automations show what each rule or schedule did and when, with the values before and after, so you can see why a campaign changed instead of guessing.

Beyond rules

Automated campaign building

Before anything runs, Create turns a short brief into a platform-ready campaign: structure, ad copy, targeting and image or video creative.

How it works

  1. Connect your ad accounts

    Connect Google Ads, Meta Ads, TikTok Ads or Pinterest Ads through each platform’s own sign-in. OrivenAI works through the official APIs; nothing is scraped.

  2. Create or pick a campaign

    Build a campaign in Create and publish it from Launch, or point a rule at a campaign that already runs in your account.

  3. Write a rule

    Choose a metric, a threshold and an action, such as “if CPA is above €40, pause the campaign”, or “if ROAS is above 4, raise the budget”.

  4. Start cautiously

    Begin with notify or approval rules. Once you trust a rule’s judgment, let it act on its own.

  5. Review the activity

    Check the Automations activity and Campaigns’ performance view to see what changed and whether it helped.

What Automations do and don’t do

Being clear about limits is part of automating safely.

What can change depends on how each campaign is set up: shared Google Ads budgets and lifetime budgets are never changed, and TikTok doesn’t report return on ad spend, so ROAS rules aren’t offered there.

Automations don’t bid in the auction or replace each platform’s own delivery systems, and they never build or publish campaigns: that is always a step you take in Launch.

Frequently asked questions

What is PPC automation software?

PPC automation software applies changes to pay-per-click campaigns automatically, based on rules or models, so that routine decisions such as pausing poor performers or reallocating budget happen without someone checking every dashboard. Good automation keeps a human in charge of the limits.

Which platforms do OrivenAI Automations support?

Automations are built for campaigns in connected Google Ads, Meta Ads, TikTok Ads and Pinterest Ads accounts. What can change depends on how each campaign is set up: shared Google Ads budgets and lifetime budgets are never changed, and TikTok doesn’t report return on ad spend, so ROAS rules aren’t offered there.

Can an automation spend more than I want?

Only through a budget rule you wrote yourself, never above the maximum budget you set, and only automatically after you confirm it may act on its own. Rules can also notify you or request approval instead of changing anything.

Are Automations included in every plan?

Yes. OrivenAI has no free plan, and every paid plan includes Automations. Creating an automation costs 20 credits once; its checks and changes don’t use credits.

Does OrivenAI replace Google’s or Meta’s own optimization?

No. Bidding and delivery still run on each platform’s systems, such as smart bidding on Google Ads. OrivenAI automates the decisions around them: which campaigns run, how budgets move, and when you get involved.
