How credits work

  • The Free plan gets 10 credits a day; paid plans get a monthly allowance.
  • An image ad campaign typically uses 100 credits (draft plus one image); a video ad campaign 225.
  • If a generation fails, its credits are returned.

Good to know

  • You can start on Free and upgrade when you need Research, Autopilot or more credits.
  • Your ad spend is paid to the ad platforms directly; OrivenAI never charges ad budgets.
  • Campaigns are created paused, so nothing spends until you switch it on.
