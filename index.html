FAQ

Questions, answered.

More on the FAQ page, or write to contact@orivenai.com.

What is OrivenAI?

OrivenAI is one connected workspace for your marketing, in six sections: Control Center for your dashboard, marketing plan and business context; Research to investigate a market; Create to build platform-ready ads; Launch to review and publish them; Campaigns to follow performance; and Automations for schedules and rules that act within your limits.

What is an OrivenAI assistant?

An assistant is the AI personality you work with in OrivenAI. There are six, each with its own specialisation, tone and character. Whichever one you choose can help you across the whole platform, not just in its specialist area. Choosing your assistant is being introduced in stages.

Do I use all six assistants at once?

No. You choose one assistant, and it stays with you as you move between research, planning, creating, launching and following results. The other five are simply different styles you could pick instead.

Can I switch assistants?

Choosing an assistant is being introduced in stages, so there is nothing to switch yet. Until then, meet all six to see which style fits you best: each one can help with the whole platform, and they differ in where they are strongest and how they talk to you.

Do I need marketing experience?

No. You start from your business profile in Control Center, and each section takes you through its step: research, create, launch and follow the results. Experienced advertisers keep full manual control over every campaign.

Which advertising platforms are supported?

Google Ads, Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram), TikTok Ads and Pinterest Ads. Every integration uses OAuth: you never share your passwords with OrivenAI, and we never store your credentials.

Can OrivenAI launch campaigns automatically?

Only the way you set it up. Campaigns you publish from Launch are created paused, so you decide when they start. Automations act only on schedules and rules you explicitly switch on, and each rule either notifies you, asks for your approval or acts within the limits you set.

Is there a free plan?

No. OrivenAI is paid-only, so there is no free plan and no free credits. You can explore every public page, the product pages and the pricing without an account. The shortest plan, Flex, gives you 14 days of full access with 1,050 credits for €17, paid once. Nothing renews automatically. See the plans.

What are credits?

Credits are the usage balance that comes with your plan. A complete image ad uses 120 credits (draft plus image), a video ad 245, a Research report 90 and an Oriven Chat message 30. They’re spent from one pool across every usage-based feature, and unused credits expire when your access ends.

How does OrivenAI handle my business data?

Your business profile, research and campaigns are stored in your account and used to fit your ads and recommendations to your business. Ad platforms connect over OAuth, so OrivenAI never sees your passwords. Advertising cookies load only with your consent. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

How it works

Check every campaign before it reaches the platform

Launch is the step between a draft and a live campaign. It shows what each platform will receive, flags what’s missing, and creates the campaign through the platform’s official API, paused.

  1. Pick a draft. Every campaign from Create waits in Launch with its readiness status.
  2. Review the settings. Objective, countries, languages, ages, placements, budget, conversion tracking, destination and tracking parameters, each marked as your choice, from your business profile, automatic, or missing.
  3. Fix what’s flagged. For example: choose a country, set a daily budget, select your Meta Pixel or Pinterest tag, confirm Meta’s special ad categories, or add a logo for Performance Max.
  4. Confirm. A summary shows exactly what will be created. Nothing is sent until you confirm.
  5. It’s created paused. Campaign, ad sets or ad groups and ads are created on the platform paused. You switch them on in the platform.

Who it’s for

  • Anyone who wants a second pair of eyes before spending money
  • Businesses running the same campaign on more than one platform
  • Teams that want publishing to be a deliberate step

Good to know

  • You always set the budget and the countries; OrivenAI never fills them in for you
  • Sales and Leads campaigns need conversion tracking on the platform (Pixel, tag or conversion actions)
  • If a platform rejects part of a campaign, everything created in that attempt is removed
  • Each platform still reviews ads under its own policies

Questions

Will my campaign start spending right away?

No. Campaigns are always created paused. Nothing spends until you activate the campaign in the ad platform.

What if I publish the same campaign twice?

Launch prevents a second publish of the same campaign while one is in progress.

Which campaign types can I launch?

Google Search, Performance Max and Demand Gen; Meta image ads; TikTok video ads for traffic and awareness; Pinterest image and video Pins.

How it works

From a short brief to a ready-to-review campaign

Create turns what you know about your product into a complete draft for one ad platform: the campaign structure, the copy for every ad, and the image or video creative, in the format that platform expects.

  1. Describe what you advertise. A product, an offer, who it’s for. Your business profile from Control Center (name, brand, website) is added automatically, and you can bring in findings from Research.
  2. Choose a platform, goal and format. Google Ads, Meta Ads, TikTok Ads or Pinterest Ads; Sales, Leads, Traffic or Awareness; image or video; and how many ad sets or ad groups and ads you want.
  3. Review the draft. Headlines, primary text, descriptions and calls to action for each ad; for Google Search, keywords, negative keywords, callouts and sitelinks. Every field is editable and shows a character counter.
  4. Generate the creative. One image or video per ad slot, sized for the platform. You can attach your own image or product photo instead.
  5. Send it to Launch. Nothing is published from Create. Launch checks the campaign and creates it on the platform paused.

Who it’s for

  • Small businesses and solo marketers who need ads for more than one platform
  • Teams that want a consistent first draft instead of a blank page
  • Anyone who wants to test several angles quickly

Good to know

  • A campaign draft uses 65 credits; each image 55, each 5-second video 180 and each 10-second video 350 (credits are refunded if generation fails)
  • Videos take a few minutes and keep generating if you leave the page
  • TikTok ads need video; the other platforms accept images
  • AI drafts are a starting point: check claims, prices and offers before publishing

Questions

Can I use my own images?

Yes. Attach an image or a product photo and Create uses it instead of generating one for that slot, so no image credits are spent on it.

Does Create publish my ads?

No. Create prepares the campaign; Launch checks it and creates it on the ad platform paused. You switch it on in the platform when you’re ready.

Which languages can I advertise in?

You write the brief in the language you want the ads in, and you can set the campaign’s target languages in its settings.

How it works

Research your market before you spend

Research answers questions like “Who are my main competitors and what do they advertise?” with a structured report you can act on, and shows which findings were checked against a source.

  1. Ask a question and choose a research type. Market, competitor, audience or opportunity research — each searches for different things and returns its own report. Add competitor pages if you want them read too.
  2. OrivenAI gathers evidence. Up to three targeted web searches using your industry and country, then it reads up to five of the most relevant pages, your own website and any pages you attached.
  3. Get a structured report. An executive summary with the key conclusions, then sections that fit the research type: competitor cards and a comparison, customer needs and objections, trends, or opportunities with suggested tests.
  4. See what each finding rests on. Findings are labelled Source-checked (a quoted passage was found in a page it read), Cited (a source was named but not checked), Analysis, or Idea to test. Source-checked findings show the quoted passage and the link.
  5. Use it. Reports are saved to your Research library. Add up to eight insights to Create as context for your next campaign.

Who it’s for

  • Businesses planning a first campaign in a new market
  • Marketers who want competitor context before writing ads
  • Anyone who needs evidence, not just AI opinions

Good to know

  • Included in every plan; each report uses 90 credits (refunded if it fails)
  • It has no access to competitors’ ad performance, budgets or results, and never claims an ad “works”
  • It doesn’t pull ads from the Meta Ad Library or Google Ads Transparency Center; it links to them so you can check real ads
  • Set your country in your business profile so findings are specific to your market

Is Research based on real data?

It runs live web searches and reads the most relevant pages, your website and any pages you attach. A finding is only marked Source-checked when the passage it quotes was found in a page it read; everything else is marked Cited, Analysis or Idea to test.

Can it show how my competitors’ ads perform?

No. No tool has access to another advertiser’s results. Research describes what competitors publicly do and say, and suggests what to test.

Where are my reports kept?

In your account, in your Research library, so you can reopen, rename or delete them on any device.

How it works

Rules you can read, actions you can predict

Automations are schedules and rules for campaigns that already run in your ad accounts: you decide the condition or the time, the action and the limits, and every action is logged.

  1. Write a rule. A condition on a metric such as CPA, ROAS, CPC, CTR, conversions or spend, for example “if CPA is above €40 over 7 days”.
  2. Choose the action and its limits. Pause or resume a campaign, adjust its budget within a range you set, notify you, or ask for your approval first.
  3. Checked for you, also when you’re offline. Schedules run at the time you set in your time zone, and rules are checked regularly on OrivenAI’s servers. Budget rules have a maximum budget and limits on how often and how much they change.
  4. Review what happened. Every run and action is recorded, so you can see why something changed.

Who it’s for

  • Advertisers who check the same numbers every day
  • Small teams that can’t watch campaigns around the clock
  • Anyone who wants automation they can explain

Good to know

  • Included in every plan; creating an automation costs 20 credits once, its checks and changes are free
  • Works with the campaigns in your connected ad accounts; shared and lifetime budgets are never changed
  • Rules only do what you wrote; no AI decides what happens
  • Approval mode lets you confirm each change before it happens

Questions

Can an automation spend more than I want?

A rule can only raise a budget up to the maximum you set, by at most the percentage you allow, and only after you confirm it may act on its own. You can also require approval before any change.

How is this different from Google’s automated bidding?

Smart Bidding sets bids inside a campaign. Automations act on whole campaigns (switch on, pause, resume, budget) at times or on conditions you choose, across your connected ad accounts.
