The problem with running ads on several platforms

Each ad platform has its own editor, its own campaign structure and its own reporting. Google Ads thinks in campaigns and ad groups around search intent; Meta, TikTok and Pinterest think in audiences and creative formats. Running all of them means rewriting the same offer four times, keeping naming and tracking consistent by hand, and stitching reports together in a spreadsheet.

OrivenAI keeps the shared parts (your business, your brand, your plan) in one place and lets each platform keep what makes it different.

What to look for in advertising management software

“Advertising management software” covers very different products. Before comparing features, check these five things, and be wary of any tool that can’t answer them plainly:

Does it act on your campaigns, or only report on them?

Reporting tools pull data from your ad accounts into one dashboard. Management tools also create, publish or change campaigns through each platform’s API. OrivenAI does both for the five platforms above: it creates and publishes campaigns, shows their results in one ads dashboard, and can pause, resume or adjust them with rules you control.

Which ad platforms does it cover?

Coverage differs a lot between tools. OrivenAI covers Google Ads, Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram), TikTok Ads, Pinterest Ads and Snapchat Ads. It doesn’t manage Microsoft Advertising, LinkedIn Ads or Amazon Ads; if those are central to your mix, choose a tool that does.

Who is it built for?

Enterprise platforms are usually sold through a sales call and priced for large budgets. Tools for small teams should let you start on your own, explain what they’ll change and show their price. OrivenAI is self-serve, with plans from €17 paid once, without automatic renewal.

How does it handle measurement?

Each platform attributes conversions its own way. Be careful with a tool that adds them up into one “true” number without explaining how. OrivenAI shows each platform’s own figures side by side and flags tracking that isn’t verified; it doesn’t merge attribution across platforms.

How much control do you keep?

Look for clear limits on what software may change, a record of what it did, and the option to approve changes first. In OrivenAI, launching is always your decision, and automations can ask before they act.

Frequently asked questions

Can I manage Google Ads and Meta Ads together in OrivenAI?

Yes. Connect both accounts, create campaigns for each from the same workspace, publish them through each platform’s API, and follow their results side by side in Campaigns.

Does OrivenAI combine conversions from different platforms into one number?

No. Google, Meta, TikTok, Pinterest and Snapchat each attribute conversions in their own way, so adding them up would double-count. OrivenAI shows each platform’s reported results side by side instead.

Do I need an existing ad account on each platform?

You connect your own ad accounts through each platform’s sign-in. Campaigns are published to, and billed by, those accounts. OrivenAI charges only for its own plan and credits.

Does OrivenAI publish campaigns automatically?

No. Launching is always a manual step. Each draft shows its readiness, and you choose when to publish.

Is cross-platform management included in every plan?

Yes. Every plan includes Control Center, Research, Create, Launch, Campaigns and Automations; plans differ only in period, price and credits.
