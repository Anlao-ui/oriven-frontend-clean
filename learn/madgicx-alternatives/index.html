Madgicx alternatives: five options compared by use case

By the OrivenAI teamPublished 12 November 20268 min read
Short answer

The best Madgicx alternative depends on why you’re switching: Meta Ads Manager’s own Advantage+ tools if you want no extra software, Birch (formerly Revealbot) for detailed automation rules on paid social, Optmyzr if Google Ads is your main channel, AdCreative.ai if you mainly need creatives, and an all-in-one platform such as OrivenAI if you run ads on Google, Meta, TikTok and Pinterest together.

Madgicx describes itself as an AI ads manager for Meta: it audits accounts, launches ads, recommends budget changes, generates creatives and reports on performance, with plans that follow your monthly ad spend. That focus on Facebook and Instagram is its strength. It is also the most common reason people look elsewhere: Madgicx’s own pages name Google Ads and TikTok as sources for its reports, not as platforms it launches and manages campaigns on.

A note on fairness: we make OrivenAI, which is one of the options below. We describe every tool by what its own website says (checked in November 2026) and don’t quote prices, because they change often. Check each vendor’s site for current details.

What to compare before switching

  • Platforms: Meta only, or also Google, TikTok, Pinterest or others, and for which jobs (launching, automating or only reporting)?
  • The job: creative production, campaign launch, automation rules, analytics and attribution, or all of them?
  • Control: can you see and approve what the software changes, and is there a log?
  • Pricing model: flat, per seat, or tied to your ad spend, and does it renew automatically?
  • Tracking: do you need server-side tracking or attribution from the tool, or do you already have it?

1. Meta Ads Manager and Advantage+: when you don’t want another tool

Meta’s own Ads Manager now automates much of what third-party tools used to do. Advantage+ options automate audiences, placements, budgets and creative variations, at no extra cost.

Consider it if you only advertise on Facebook and Instagram and mainly used Madgicx for its recommendations. Keep in mind that Meta optimizes for its own delivery, and you won’t get rules, reports or creative help that work across other platforms.

2. Birch (formerly Revealbot): when you want detailed automation rules

Birch is an ad automation platform for paid social. Its site describes automated rules with nested conditions and custom metrics, bulk launching of creative tests, and reporting, on Meta, Google Ads, TikTok and Snapchat. It offers a self-serve free trial, and its plans depend on ad spend.

Consider it if you run many campaigns or accounts and want fine-grained rules and bulk launching. Keep in mind that it is built for teams that already produce their own creatives and run campaigns at volume.

3. Optmyzr: when Google Ads is your main channel

Optmyzr is PPC management software for agencies, freelancers and in-house teams, centred on Google Ads. It lists Microsoft Ads, Amazon Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads and TikTok Ads among its supported platforms, with a rule engine, account audits, bid and budget tools and reporting.

Consider it if your spend is mostly on search and shopping campaigns and you want deep optimization tools. Keep in mind that its depth is in paid search; it is less suited if Meta creative is where your work is.

4. AdCreative.ai: when creatives are the bottleneck

AdCreative.ai focuses on generating ad creatives and ad text from your brand assets. If you used Madgicx mainly for its AI creative features, a dedicated creative tool may fit better. Our comparison of AdCreative.ai alternatives covers this group of tools.

Consider it if producing ads takes most of your time. Keep in mind that creative tools don’t manage or automate your campaigns.

5. OrivenAI: when you advertise on more than Meta

OrivenAI is an AI advertising platform for small teams that run ads on several networks. You research your market, create a campaign with copy and creative from a short brief, check and publish it from Launch to Google Ads, Meta Ads, TikTok Ads or Pinterest Ads through each platform’s official API, follow the results in Campaigns, and let rules you control pause, resume or adjust budgets. Plans are paid once, from €17 for 14 days, without automatic renewal.

Consider it if you want one workspace for several ad platforms rather than a specialist for one. Keep in mind that it goes less deep on Meta than a Meta-only tool: it has no attribution model or ongoing server-side tracking of its own (it can create a Meta Pixel and send a test event), it publishes image ads on Meta but not video ads yet, and it doesn’t manage bids.

How to decide

  1. List the platforms you advertise on now, and the ones you plan to add this year.
  2. Write down what you actually used Madgicx for: creatives, rules, launching or reports.
  3. Shortlist the two options whose main purpose matches that list, and use their trials on one real campaign.
  4. Compare how much of the work each one removed, not the length of its feature list.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a Madgicx alternative that covers Google, TikTok and Pinterest?

Yes. Birch manages Meta, Google Ads, TikTok and Snapchat; Optmyzr is centred on Google Ads with Meta, TikTok and others; OrivenAI creates, launches and follows campaigns on Google Ads, Meta Ads, TikTok Ads and Pinterest Ads. Check each vendor for the exact jobs it supports per platform.

Is there a free alternative to Madgicx?

Meta Ads Manager and its Advantage+ features cost nothing beyond your ad spend. Most third-party tools are paid; OrivenAI is paid-only, from €17 for 14 days, paid once without automatic renewal.

Does OrivenAI replace Madgicx’s attribution and tracking?

No. OrivenAI shows each platform’s own reported results side by side and flags tracking that isn’t verified, but it doesn’t run ongoing server-side tracking or its own attribution model. It can create a Meta Pixel and send a test server event; your website still needs the Pixel or a Conversions API integration. If you rely on Madgicx’s tracking, keep a dedicated tracking tool.
