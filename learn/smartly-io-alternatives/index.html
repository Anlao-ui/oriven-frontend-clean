Smartly.io alternatives for small teams: five options compared

By the OrivenAI teamPublished 12 November 20268 min read
Short answer

Smartly.io is an enterprise platform, so most alternatives for small teams are self-serve tools that do part of what it does: the ad platforms’ own tools if you want no extra cost, Madgicx for Meta, Birch for paid-social automation, Optmyzr for search-heavy accounts, and an all-in-one workspace such as OrivenAI to create, launch and follow campaigns on Google, Meta, TikTok and Pinterest.

Smartly calls itself an AI advertising platform that brings creative and media together across channels. Its site describes three suites (creative production, media buying and automation, and intelligence and reporting), lists channels from Meta, Google, TikTok and Pinterest to Snapchat, Amazon, connected TV and the open web, and shows large brands and agencies as customers. It doesn’t publish prices; you start by booking a demo.

That makes it a strong fit for large advertisers with creative and media teams, and a heavy one for a small business or a lean marketing team. This guide compares alternatives for that second group. We make OrivenAI, one of the options below; every tool is described by what its own website says (checked in November 2026), without prices, which change often.

What a small team actually needs from Smartly’s feature list

  • Channels: most small advertisers run two to four platforms, typically Google and Meta, sometimes TikTok or Pinterest. Connected TV and programmatic display are rarely part of the mix.
  • Creative: enough ad variations per platform, without a design team.
  • Launching: campaigns set up correctly the first time, in each platform’s structure.
  • Monitoring: one place to see results, and rules that catch problems overnight.
  • Buying: a price you can see and a way to start without a sales call.

1. The ad platforms’ own tools: when budget is the main constraint

Meta Ads Manager (with Advantage+), Google Ads (with Performance Max) and TikTok Ads Manager each automate audiences, placements and bidding, and increasingly help with creative, at no cost beyond your ad spend.

Consider them if you run one or two platforms and have time to work in each editor. Keep in mind that nothing is shared between them: briefs, naming, reports and rules are repeated per platform.

2. Madgicx: when Meta is your main channel

Madgicx is an AI ads manager for Meta: account audits, ad launching, budget recommendations, AI creatives and analytics, with a free trial and plans that follow your ad spend. Its reports can include Google Ads and TikTok data. Our Madgicx alternatives guide covers it in more detail.

Consider it if most of your spend is on Facebook and Instagram. Keep in mind that it manages and launches campaigns on Meta only.

3. Birch (formerly Revealbot): when you need automation at volume

Birch is an ad automation platform for paid social: detailed rules, bulk launching of creative tests and reporting on Meta, Google Ads, TikTok and Snapchat. It is self-serve, with a free trial and published plans that depend on ad spend.

Consider it if you already produce creatives and want Smartly-style automation without an enterprise contract. Keep in mind that it doesn’t create the ads for you.

4. Optmyzr: when search is the bigger part of your spend

Optmyzr is PPC management software centred on Google Ads, also supporting Microsoft Ads, Amazon Ads, Meta, LinkedIn and TikTok. It offers rules, account audits, bid and budget tools and reporting, with a self-serve trial.

Consider it if Google Search, Shopping and Performance Max carry your account. Keep in mind that it is built for optimizing campaigns, not for producing social creative.

5. OrivenAI: when you want one workspace for four platforms

OrivenAI is an AI advertising platform for small teams. It covers the steps Smartly splits across suites, at a smaller scale: research your market and competitors, create a campaign with copy and creative from a short brief, check and publish it from Launch to Google Ads, Meta Ads, TikTok Ads or Pinterest Ads through each platform’s official API, follow results in one ads dashboard, and let rules you control handle routine changes. It is self-serve, with plans from €17 for 14 days, paid once without automatic renewal.

Consider it if you want the create-launch-follow loop on several platforms without assembling separate tools. Keep in mind what it doesn’t do: no Snapchat, Amazon, LinkedIn, connected TV or programmatic channels; no creative services or account team; Meta ads are published as image ads (not video yet) and TikTok campaigns for traffic and awareness; and no attribution model of its own.

How to decide

  1. Write down which channels carry at least a tenth of your ad spend. Ignore the rest for now.
  2. Decide whether your bottleneck is making ads, launching them, or watching them.
  3. Shortlist the two options that cover those channels and that bottleneck, and test each on one real campaign.
  4. If you grow into connected TV, programmatic or a creative team of your own, an enterprise platform like Smartly may become worth the demo.

Frequently asked questions

Does Smartly.io have a self-serve plan for small businesses?

Its website doesn’t show one: there are no published prices and the main call to action is to book a demo. Check with Smartly for current options.

Which Smartly alternative covers Pinterest?

Of the tools in this guide, OrivenAI creates, publishes and follows Pinterest campaigns, alongside Google Ads, Meta Ads and TikTok Ads. Pinterest’s own Ads Manager is the native option.

Is OrivenAI a full replacement for Smartly?

Not for a large advertiser. OrivenAI covers four ad platforms and the core loop of research, creating, launching, following and automating campaigns for small teams. It doesn’t offer Smartly’s wider channel list, creative services or enterprise support.
